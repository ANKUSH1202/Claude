## 1. Master prompt (hero banner)

```
Create a premium Amazon A+ content banner image, exact size 970 x 600 pixels
(landscape, 97:60 aspect ratio), for a baby toy called [PRODUCT NAME].

PRODUCT: [PRODUCT DESCRIPTION], made from [MATERIALS], designed for babies
aged [AGE RANGE]. Show the product exactly as it is, sharp and in focus,
true-to-life colors, placed on the right 55% of the frame.

SCENE: a bright, airy, modern nursery. Soft natural window light from the
left, gentle shadows, a cozy play mat or light wooden floor, a few soft
props (knit blanket, wooden blocks) kept blurred in the background.
A happy, smiling baby of the correct age range is playing safely with the
toy under natural supervision.

COLOR & MOOD: warm, calm, joyful and trustworthy. Pastel palette built
around [BRAND COLORS]. Clean, uncluttered, premium lifestyle photography,
shallow depth of field (f/2.8 look), 50mm lens perspective.

LAYOUT: keep the left 40% calm with soft negative space for headline text
added later. Keep 40 px safe margin on all edges. Nothing important cut off.

STYLE: photorealistic, high-end commercial product photography, 4K detail,
soft color grading, no harsh contrast.

AVOID: any text, logos, watermarks, borders, distorted hands or faces,
extra fingers, small detachable parts near the baby's mouth, unsafe
sleeping positions, cluttered backgrounds, oversaturated colors,
competitor brands.
```

## 2. Supporting module prompts (same 970 × 600 size)

Use the master prompt above and swap the **SCENE / LAYOUT** part with one of these.

### Module 2 — Key features callout
```
Clean studio shot of [PRODUCT NAME] centered on a seamless soft
[BRAND COLOR] background. Product shown at a slight 3/4 angle with soft
shadow. Leave even empty space around the product on both sides for
3–4 feature callout icons and labels to be added later. Minimal, bright,
premium e-commerce look.
```

### Module 3 — Developmental benefits (lifestyle)
```
Close-up lifestyle photo: a baby aged [AGE RANGE] grasping / exploring
[PRODUCT NAME] while lying on a soft play mat during tummy time; parent's
hands gently visible at the edge of the frame. Focus on the baby's
curiosity and the textures of the toy. Negative space on the top-left
for text.
```

### Module 4 — Safety & materials
```
Macro detail shot of [PRODUCT NAME] showing the texture and quality of
[MATERIALS] — smooth rounded edges, soft stitching, no small parts.
Natural light, calm neutral background, clean and reassuring mood.
Space on the right side for "safety" badges to be added later.
```

arranged on a light wooden or cream surface, evenly spaced, with a
parent's hand or common object for size reference. Soft even lighting,
no shadows clipping, plenty of breathing space.

## 4. Amazon A+ checklist for baby toys

- Export at **exactly 970 × 600 px**, JPG or PNG, under 2 MB, sRGB.
- Add text in a design tool afterwards — AI-generated text is often misspelled.
- Keep text at least 24 px font size so it is readable on mobile.
- **Do not** use claims Amazon rejects: "best", "#1", "top-rated", "safest",
  "guaranteed", pricing, discounts, shipping info, or reviews/quotes.
- Only show safety certifications (e.g. ASTM F963, CPSIA, EN71) if you
  have the documentation.
- Show the correct age grading and choking-hazard warnings where required.
- Baby must always be shown safely: supervised, awake during play, no toys
  in cribs with sleeping babies.
- Do not mention competitors or link to off-Amazon websites.
